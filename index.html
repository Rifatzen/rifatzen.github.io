About me

I'm Rifat, a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at BRAC University, Dhaka. I graduated from the Department of CSE at BUET in 2025 with a CGPA of 3.90/4.00.

My undergraduate thesis, New Results on Unique Neighbourhood Networks, is on a graph class in which no two vertices share the same neighbourhood. I derived a new formula for counting Unique Neighbourhood Networks on a given number of vertices, and used that formula to enumerate them. Graph theory and combinatorics are where most of my interest sits.

Outside of that I build things end to end — a conference management system in Svelte and Supabase, a C compiler with Flex and Bison, a ray tracer in C++, a neural network from scratch in Python, and a handful of hardware projects from logic gates up to a working MIPS simulation. I also competed in mathematics olympiads for most of a decade.
